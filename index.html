The Real Challenge

Healthcare is hard to navigate — for everyone involved.

Patients struggle with confusing systems and scattered records. Doctors and providers struggle to stay connected and coordinate care. AYU addresses both sides.

For Patients & Families

Reports are hard to understand

Diagnostic test results are written in technical medical jargon. Families spend hours worrying without knowing whether a value is safe or serious.

Medicines and follow-ups get missed

Managing multiple pills across before-food and after-food timings is confusing. Doses get skipped, doubled, or taken at the wrong time.

Records are scattered everywhere

Prescriptions, lab slips, and discharge summaries are buried across plastic files, paper folders, and phone galleries — impossible to locate at a new clinic.

Emergencies increase stress

During an urgent medical event, there is no time to remember app passwords or search paper stacks for allergy history and active medications.

For Doctors & Providers

Patients miss reminders and follow-ups

After appointments, patients often forget instructions, miss follow-up schedules, and skip prescribed tests — reducing treatment effectiveness.

Medical communication is often hard to understand

Many patients can't fully understand written prescriptions, dosage instructions, or post-consultation notes — especially when delivered in English or clinical language.

Patients are uncomfortable with apps and portals

Operational coordination becomes harder when patients — especially older adults — are unfamiliar or uncomfortable with dedicated health apps and online portals.

Continuity of care is difficult to maintain

Accessibility and continuity suffer when patients can't easily share records, track medication history, or communicate between visits.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about AYU — whether you're a patient, caregiver, doctor, or healthcare organization.

Do I need to install any new app to use AYU?

No. AYU works completely within WhatsApp. You do not need to download anything from the App Store or Play Store, memorize new passwords, or update separate software. This applies to both patients and healthcare providers.

Which languages does AYU support for voice notes?

AYU supports English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, and Bengali. You can switch your preferred language at any time simply by telling AYU on WhatsApp.

How does the loved ones' voice reminder feature work?

A family member (son, daughter, or caregiver) can send a 5-second voice note to AYU saying "Mom, time for your evening medicine". AYU pairs this recording with the scheduled medication alert so your parent hears your warm, familiar voice every day.

Is AYU free for patients and families?

Yes. AYU is completely free for individual patients and families to understand reports, receive reminders, and organize records. We partner directly with clinics, diagnostic centers, and pharmacies to deliver connected care.

I'm a doctor. How does AYU help with patient follow-up?

AYU provides a communication layer on WhatsApp where your post-visit instructions, lab report explanations, and medication reminders reach patients in their local language. It reduces repeat calls, improves medication adherence, and ensures patients arrive at follow-ups better prepared.

Can my clinic, hospital, or lab integrate with AYU?

Yes. We are expanding our partner network across India. Clinics can connect patient OPD workflows, labs can send automated digital reports with voice summaries, and pharmacies can sync refill reminders. Contact our partner team at partners@ayuhealth.in or message us on WhatsApp.

How does AYU protect health data and privacy?

All WhatsApp conversations and documents are protected with WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption standards. Your health records are indexed privately and are never sold or shared with advertisers.
