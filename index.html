The Problem

The outpatient experience is broken.

Millions of patients visit hospital OPDs every day in India. Most of them face the same frustrations — and most hospitals lack the systems to fix it.

Excessive waiting

Patients spend hours in crowded lobbies with no visibility into when they'll be seen. Time that could be spent resting or at work.

Chaotic OPD flow

Queues are managed manually or not at all. Staff juggle token systems, paper registers, and constant interruptions throughout the day.

Fragmented communication

Updates come via phone calls, WhatsApp, or word of mouth. There's no single channel for appointments, reminders, and follow-ups.

Stressful for everyone

It's not just patients. Doctors lose consultation time. Receptionists are overwhelmed. The system creates friction at every step.

The Vision

A calmer, smarter way to access care.

Ayu is not another hospital app. It's the coordination layer that makes OPD visits predictable, organized, and less stressful — for every stakeholder.

Smoother care access

Book, check in, and track your visit without standing in line.

Queue and visit coordination

Real-time queue visibility so you arrive when it's your turn — not hours before.

Unified communication

Appointments, reports, and updates in one place — no more scattered messages.

Why Now

The timing is right.

Healthcare access in India is evolving. The infrastructure hasn't caught up — but the conditions for change are here.

01

Digital expectations are rising

Patients now book cabs, food, and flights on their phones. They expect the same from healthcare — but hospital systems haven't kept pace.

02

Hospitals need better operations

Even well-funded hospitals struggle with OPD congestion and manual coordination. The problem isn't awareness — it's tooling.

03

Communication must simplify

Health interactions are scattered across calls, messages, and paper. One unified channel would reduce errors and improve follow-through.
