The Burden

A 100km journey for a 15-minute consultation.

For patients traveling from distant towns, reaching the hospital is only the first hurdle. The overhead inside the outpatient department consumes their day, savings, and energy.

Long-distance travel

Patients and their family members travel hours by bus or train, sacrificing a daily wage and paying for transit just to get clinical access.

Blind lobby waiting

They stand in lines to register, pay, and get tokens. They wait for hours in congested corridors without knowing when their doctor is ready.

Brief consultations

After a full day spent traveling and waiting, the actual slot with the specialist lasts only 15 to 20 minutes before they start their trip back.

Repeat report trips

If diagnostics are ordered, patients must return another day, pay for travel again, and lose another day of work simply to show paper reports to the doctor.

How it works

Zero new apps.
Built entirely on WhatsApp.

Ayu connects the hospital's internal workflow systems with the messaging app patients already use every day. We make outpatient coordination simple, conversational, and direct.

Direct check-in & token setup

Scan a QR code at the desk or text the hospital number to register and confirm arrival instantly.

Just-in-time turn updates

Live queue status tells you how many people are ahead, letting you wait in comfort elsewhere.

UPI fee payments in chat

Receive diagnostic and billing links in the thread, make secure payments, and receive immediate receipts.

Digital reports on demand

Lab results and digital prescription copies are delivered as PDFs inside the thread as soon as they are ready.

Why Now

The infrastructure must adapt.

Patients don't need another medical portal or heavy app. They need real-time operational transparency in the application they already use daily.

01

Ubiquity of WhatsApp

Virtually every family in India communicates via WhatsApp. By delivering tokens and reports directly to the chat window, we eliminate the friction of app downloads.

02

Rising cost of travel overhead

Failing to optimize visit times translates directly to lost daily wages and expensive transport fares. Minimizing wait times is an economic necessity.

03

Lobby density fatigue

Crowded waiting areas generate noise, confusion, and operational blockages. Moving check-ins and test reporting online relieves physical hospital spaces.
